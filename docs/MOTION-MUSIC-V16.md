# Motion Music v16

This update fixes the beat pulse and the visualiser stage shortcut, makes every visualiser smoother and living gradients easy to see, adds five more visualisers, and gives My Wrapped badges, a listening calendar, a throwback, your favourite albums, a genre mix and a share picture.

## Open this version

1. Fully quit the old Motion Music using **Quit** in the system tray or **Ctrl+Q**.
2. Extract the Windows ZIP and open **Motion Music v16.exe**.
3. Keep **spotifast-portable.txt** beside the app, and move your existing **spotifast-data** folder beside it to keep your settings, sign-in and personal Spotify app.

## Fixed

- **The visualiser stage opens:** it is now **Ctrl+Shift+S**, because Windows turns Ctrl+Shift+V into a paste before the app sees it. There is also an expand button in the player bar.
- **Beat pulse works:** the detector judged each beat against an average that, on a high-refresh screen, caught up with the kick before it could stand out. It is now timed by the clock, isolates the bass more sharply, and still follows quiet listening. Each beat also flashes more clearly: a bigger ring, a taller glow and a warm wash over the player bar, and a gentle flash on the stage.
- **Smoother visualisers:** the scenes glide between spectra instead of jumping as each one arrives, and an uncapped frame rate now stays at 60 frames a second, rather than 30, when Motion Music is behind another window.
- **Living gradients you can see:** the colours now drift about four times faster, once every twenty seconds or so. Solid themes get a gentle gradient of their accent colour while the setting is on, so they drift too.

## Five more visualisers

| Visualiser | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| Matrix rain | Columns of glowing marks falling faster with their part of the spectrum |
| Spectrogram | A scrolling heat map of the music, low notes at the bottom |
| Neon orb | A spinning wireframe globe inside a ring of light, swelling with the bass |
| Rose curves | Three layered flowers whose petals reach out with the spectrum |
| Bubbles | Bubbles rising and swelling with their part of the spectrum |

That makes twenty-six. They all work on the stage too.

## More in My Wrapped

- **Badges** for how you listen: Night owl, Early bird, Superfan, Explorer, On repeat, Weekend warrior, On a streak and Marathon listener. Point at one to see why you earned it.
- **Your listening calendar**: the last twelve weeks, one square a day, brighter for more music.
- **A week ago today**: the song you played most that day.
- **Albums you loved**: your three most played albums. Click one to open it.
- **Your sound**: the genres of your top artists as bars.
- **Save as image** puts the top of the page, from the title to the calendar, in **Pictures\Motion Music** as a PNG, ready to share.
- Motion Music now remembers your last 2,000 plays on this computer instead of 500, so the stats cover more of your listening.
